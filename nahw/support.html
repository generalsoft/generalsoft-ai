Help & support

How can we help?

If you have a question about Nahw, found something that doesn't work as expected, or have a suggestion for improving the app, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact us

The quickest way to reach us is by email. Please include as much detail as possible about the issue, including your device and the version of Nahw you're using when relevant.

support@generalsoft.ae

Bug reports

If you've found a bug, please tell us what you were doing when it happened and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are especially helpful when reporting visual or layout problems.

Suggestions

Nahw is continually being improved. If there's a feature, translation, grammar tool, or study workflow you'd like to see, send us your suggestion.

App availability

Nahw is available through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Now available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android. Visit the home page for the current download links.

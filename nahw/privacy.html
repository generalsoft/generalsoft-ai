Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Overview

Nahw is a Qur'an grammar and word study application developed by Generalsoft FZ-LLC. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the information we collect limited to what is necessary to operate and improve our services.

Information collected by the website

The Nahw website does not require you to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse the site.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics on the Nahw website to understand how visitors use the site and to help us improve its content and user experience.

Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, approximate location, device and browser information, referral information, and interactions with the website. Google may use cookies or similar technologies for this purpose.

Analytics measurement ID: G-86LWCL4FNH

We use Google Analytics for website measurement and analysis. We do not intentionally send information to Google Analytics that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, password, or similar personally identifying information.

App privacy

Nahw is designed as a focused Qur'anic study application and does not require an account to use its core study features. The information and data available inside the app may differ depending on the platform and version of the application.

For platform-specific information about data handling, please also review the privacy information provided by the Apple App Store or Google Play for the version of Nahw you install.

Third-party services

The Nahw website may link to third-party services, including the Apple App Store and Google Play. When you follow those links, your interaction is subject to the privacy practices and policies of those third parties.

Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies through third-party services such as Google Analytics. These technologies help us understand website usage and improve the site.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly use the website to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the date shown at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Nahw's privacy practices, please contact us at support@generalsoft.ae.
